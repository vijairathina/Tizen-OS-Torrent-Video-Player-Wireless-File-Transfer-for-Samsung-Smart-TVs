🛡️ Samsung TV Ad-Block & Performance Guide

Follow these 3 simple TV settings steps to permanently stop home screen banner ads, disable telemetry tracking, and make your Samsung Smart TV run at peak speed:

1
Turn Off "Autorun Smart Hub" (Stop Lag on Startup)
Go to Settings → General & Privacy → Smart Features and turn OFF Autorun Smart Hub and Autorun Last App. Your TV will boot instantly without loading heavy ad ribbons!
2
Disable IBA & ACR Viewing Data Tracking
Go to Settings → General & Privacy → Terms & Privacy → Privacy Choices. Uncheck Interest-Based Advertisements (IBA) and Viewing Information Services. This stops background video scanning and profile tracking.
3
Set AdGuard Ad-Blocking DNS on TV
Go to Settings → Network → Network Status → IP Settings → DNS Setting → Enter Manually.
Enter DNS: 94.140.14.14 (Secondary: 94.140.15.15). This blocks 100% of Samsung Smart Hub ads and TV Plus video ads!
